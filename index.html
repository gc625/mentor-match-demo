CSV format & matching rules +

One person per row. First column is the name; remaining columns are ranked choices. Use an optional Name,Preferences header. Names are case-sensitive and must exactly match the other file. Empty lists are allowed.

Name,Preferences
Amira,"Sofia, Julian, Maya"
Daniel,Noah,Priya,Sofia

Standard CSV quoting works for person names with commas. To include a comma inside a ranked name, quote that name inside the preference cell and escape its quotes in the outer CSV, e.g. Amira,"""Reyes, Sofia"",Julian". Empty items within a quoted list, unknown names and duplicates need correction; lists may be empty, and blank separate columns are ignored.

Mutual consent: a pair is eligible only if each person ranked the other. Unranked or one-sided choices are never assigned. Each mentor and mentee can have at most one partner; unmatched people remain visible.

Optimization: first maximize the number of eligible pairs. Among maximum-size matchings, maximize total mutual score. Each side contributes round(1000 × (list length − zero-based rank) / list length); the pair score is their sum. Hungarian assignment finds the global optimum. Ties are resolved deterministically by input order, not a fairness guarantee.

Stability: the optional check finds blocking pairs: two people who both ranked each other and both strictly prefer each other to their assignment (or being unmatched). Optimal score does not guarantee stability; the check does not change the matching.
